Cali Fund

Disyn Biotec has joined the Cali Fund, the UN's global fund for sharing the benefits of genetic sequence data.

Why we joined

Sequence data from nature is at the heart of what we do across all aspects of our company, from our service offering through to our digital tools.

Enzyme discovery: we build bespoke enzyme panels by mining sequence space for diverse or structurally promising candidates.

Enzyme engineering: sequence and structure data guide which residues we target in each round of directed evolution.

AI and digital tools: RetroBioCat, Biocat Copilot, and our machine-learning models are built on published enzyme sequences and reaction data.

Our commitment

We have signed a revenue-sharing agreement with the Cali Fund. In doing so we are one of the first organizations worldwide contributing to the Cali Fund, which includes our long-standing enzyme supply partner Prozomix. It is a small contribution today, but as we grow, so will our share, and we hope more biotechnology companies will join us.
