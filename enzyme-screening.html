Enzyme Screening

Rapid screening of enzyme panels to find the most suitable candidates for your biocatalytic processes.

With access to a whole host of analytical techniques, including plate-readers, UPLC-DAD, UPLC-ELSD, LC-MS, GC-FID & GC-MS, we can rapidly screen enzyme panels to find the most suitable candidates for your biocatalytic processes.

In addition, we work in partnership with Prozomix to access their biocatalyst panels, currently >6000 enzymes spanning multiple enzyme classes. We can quickly screen their enzyme panels against your substrate to identify a starting enzyme for your reaction/process.

We also offer bespoke enzyme panel development, with a range of bioinformatics techniques available to select enzymes for testing, from maximising diversity with a given sequence space, through to more targeted structure guided approaches.
